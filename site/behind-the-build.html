What the development labels mean

READING THE STATUS LABELS

Know what a test actually proves.

Approved design

A selected visual direction. It may be artwork or a simulated interface rather than a working operational path.

Implemented

The behavior exists in code. Real-equipment tests, installation and full testing may still be needed.

Recorded tests

A dated test supports a specific result. Where it ran and any known failures still matter.

Experimental

An investigation or integration exists but important device, meaning, delivery or reliability gates remain.

Planned

A documented intention or design. It is not presented as a completed feature.

Unknown or unverified

Available evidence does not establish the claim. The right response is to name the gap and define the next check.

LESSONS ACROSS THE WORK

MINING COMMAND

A dashboard is not a supervisor.

Monitoring, automatic safeguards and owner-approved actions need separate responsibilities and records.

DECISION DESK

A quote is not an execution.

Freshness, modeled fills, account identity and recovery determine whether a result can be trusted.

FITNESS COMPANION

A reading is not just a number.

Profile, unit, timing and overlap determine whether an observation belongs in a trend or daily total.
