MrMagic Fitness Beta

Privacy policy · Updated November 9, 2026

Scope and contact

This policy covers MrMagic Fitness Beta (com.mrmagic.MagicFitnessBeta). For support or privacy questions, contact mrmagicsuite@gmail.com. Please use made-up examples or redact sensitive values; do not email health records, passwords or backup files.

Records stay in this beta

Workout entries, nutrition logs, manual body entries, exercise drafts and preferences are processed locally in this beta app’s separate container. No account, developer database, app cloud synchronization, advertising, analytics SDK or AI service is included. A fresh installation contains no owner history or device profile. Example goals are not personal recommendations.

Apple Health and devices are off

This beta cannot request Apple Health permission or read, export or write HealthKit records. Live scale, watch, Bluetooth and Spotify/account integrations and incoming URL imports are excluded from this build. Manual logging works without those connections. Missing readings remain unavailable, not zero.

Ask MrMagic

Ask provides deterministic, on-device how-to help. It does not query personal records, retain questions, diagnose conditions or send questions to an AI provider.

Automatic backups and device protection

The beta marks its database/drafts directory and its own Preferences directory as excluded from automatic device backups. This includes preferences that can contain body goals. There is no app-managed iCloud database or CloudKit synchronization. iOS file protection is requested for those directories; device security and backup behavior also depend on iOS. Do not rely on automatic recovery of beta records.

Manual exports and imports

Export creates an unencrypted JSON backup containing your beta records, drafts, extra sets and preferences. You choose the Files destination; that provider handles any copy you save there. For beta testing, use made-up records and a private local destination such as On My iPhone. Do not save health records to iCloud or share them as feedback. The app cannot control exported copies or independently verify a chosen provider’s retention. Import accepts compatible Fitness Beta backups only and rejects personal-app, M2 Review and legacy backup identifiers before merging.

TestFlight and feedback

For a TestFlight release, Apple processes installation/testing information, crashes and feedback under its TestFlight terms and privacy practices. Feedback and screenshots you deliberately send may be available to the developer. Send made-up examples or redacted screenshots only. The app does not automatically upload your workout database or send reports to the support mailbox. Review Apple’s TestFlight privacy information before accepting its invitation.

Links and this policy website

External references open only when you choose them; those providers have separate privacy practices. The proposed GitHub Pages policy site uses no embedded analytics, cookies, forms, tracking scripts or remote assets in this page. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security. Visiting the policy page does not give the app or developer access to your fitness records.

Retention and deletion

Records remain in the beta’s local container until you delete them using available app or iOS controls. Removing only this beta removes its local app data, not the separate personal app or Apple Health originals. Exported copies, support email and Apple feedback are separate: remove exported copies at their destination and contact support about developer-held correspondence. Apple manages its own TestFlight records under its policies. No fixed deletion deadline for those independent services is promised.

Testing boundaries

This is a fitness log, not medical advice, a diagnosis or a prescribed workout. Extra sets are retained separately and do not enter the legacy two-set totals; the known workout-count regression remains disclosed. Exercise photographs with unclear redistribution rights, personal instructions and unapproved sayings are omitted. Full physical-device accessibility and release acceptance remain incomplete. Report problems with synthetic examples and stop if the app behaves unexpectedly.
